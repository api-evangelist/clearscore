# ClearScore

> ClearScore is a London-founded fintech (2015) that gives consumers free access to their credit score and report and matches them to financial products — credit cards, loans, car finance, insurance and more — through a personalised financial marketplace. It also runs ClearScore Protect (dark-web identity monitoring), DriveScore (driving-behaviour scoring) and Open Banking features, and operates in the UK, South Africa, Australia, Canada and New Zealand. Its B2B arm, ClearScore Connect, exposes credit-data and eligibility services to partner lenders through a partner-gated developer portal. Backed by QED Investors.
